*Let Figr read your design system straight from your codebase, so its designs match what's actually built.*

Your code is the final word on how your product looks: real tokens, real components, real styles. Connect your repo and Figr builds your design system from it, so designs line up with what your engineers ship.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new design system">
    Open **Design System** in the sidebar and create a new one, or open an existing one to add a source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your repo as a source">
    Under **Sources**, choose **GitHub** and select the repo, and the folder where your design system lives if it's not at the root.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Upload Code source with the Add GitHub option"
        src="/images/docs/repo-02-select.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it up">
    Click **Setup design system**. Figr reads your tokens, components and styles and adds them to your design system.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Not on GitHub?** You can also upload your theme or style files, like CSS or token files, directly as a source.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Point Figr at the folder that holds your design tokens and shared components, not the whole app. A focused source gives cleaner results.
</Tip>
