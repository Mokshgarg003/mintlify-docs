*No full design system yet? Give Figr images of your brand, and it'll keep every design on-brand.*

You don't need a complete component library to get on-brand results. Your logo, icons and a few screenshots of your product or site are enough for Figr to match your look from the first message.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new design system">
    Open **Design System** in the sidebar and create a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose brand assets as a source">
    Under **Sources**, choose **Brand assets**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Sources list with Brand assets selected"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-1.56.42-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your images">
    Add your logo, icons and a few screenshots of your product or site. Brand assets take images only: PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG or GIF.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Uploaded brand assets listed in the design system"
        src="/images/docs/brand-03-uploaded.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it up">
    Click **Setup design system**. Figr reads your images and builds a design system you can use right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Five screenshots give Figr a basic read of your style. Around 20 give it a much deeper one.
</Tip>

<Info>
  **Even quicker:** if you don't attach a design system at all, Figr can pick up your brand colours and type from your website automatically.
</Info>
