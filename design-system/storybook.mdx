_Point Figr at your Storybook, so it designs with the components your engineers already ship._

If your components live in Storybook, that's the most accurate picture of your design system: real components, real props and real states. Figr connects to it through your published Storybook MCP server, so what it designs maps to what your team can build.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new design system">
    Open **Design System** in the sidebar and create a new one, or open an existing one to add a source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Storybook as a source">
    Under **Sources**, choose **Storybook**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Sources list with Storybook selected"
        src="/images/docs/sb-02-source.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your Storybook MCP server URL">
    Paste the URL of your published Storybook MCP server, then click **Add connector**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Connect Storybook dialog with the MCP server URL field"
        src="/images/docs/sb-03-url.png"
      />
    </Frame>

    <Note>
      Only a published Storybook MCP server works here. A regular Storybook site URL won't connect.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it up">
    Click **Setup design system**. Figr reads your components, their variants and states, and adds them to your design system.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Make sure each component has stories for its main variants and states, like default, hover, disabled and error. Figr can only use what your stories show.
</Tip>
