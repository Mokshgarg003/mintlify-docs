*Let Figr make changes to components in your Figma file, so your design system and your designs stay in sync.*

When you refine a component in Figr, like a new button state or an updated card, you don't have to rebuild it by hand in Figma. Figr can update your Figma file directly, using your existing components and variables.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Figma">
    Click **Figma Sync** in the top-right nav bar and connect the [Figr AI Figma Sync](https://www.figma.com/community/plugin/1354402282325383952/figr-ai-figma-sync) plugin to your design system file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the components to change">
    In Figma, select the components or frames you want Figr to update. They're added to the chat box automatically.

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 10 21 At 2 06 01 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-2.06.01-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 10 21 At 2 06 01 PM")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the change">
    Tell Figr what to update, for example: "Add a loading state to the primary button, using our spinner icon and existing colour variables."

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Chat box with a prompt describing a component change"
        src="/images/docs/upd-03-prompt.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result in Figma">
    Figr updates the components in your file and links them to your existing variables. Anything it can't link stays a regular frame, with a note explaining why.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Try changes in a branch or a copy of your design system file first, so your team's main library only changes once you're happy with it.
</Tip>
