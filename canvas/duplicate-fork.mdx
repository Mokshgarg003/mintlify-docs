*Try a new direction without losing what you have.*

Duplicate a screen or flow when you want a copy to change, and fork when you want to explore a different direction from where you are now. Either way, your original stays exactly as it was.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Duplicate a screen or node">
    Select it on the canvas and choose **Duplicate**. The copy appears next to the original, ready to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fork to explore a new direction">
    Choose **Fork** to start a new branch of the work from this point. Ask Figr for changes in the fork without touching the original.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare side by side">
    Keep the original and the copy or fork next to each other on the canvas so you and your team can compare them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the one that works">
    Delete or move the version you don't need, or keep both in separate sections.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Rename copies and forks straight away, for example "Checkout – option B", so it's always clear which version is which.
</Tip>
