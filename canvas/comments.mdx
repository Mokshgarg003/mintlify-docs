*Give feedback right where it applies.*

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/EhnWaFaZ744" title="Build and review together in Figr" frameBorder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowFullScreen />

Comments let your team discuss a design in context. Pin a comment to a screen or element, mention a teammate, and resolve the thread once it's handled, so feedback doesn't get lost in chat or email. Figr also reads comments on the canvas as context on its next turn.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a comment">
    Choose the comment tool in the left toolbar and click the spot on the canvas you want to talk about.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Comment tool in the left toolbar"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-3.49.07-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="New comment placed on a screen on the canvas"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-3.48.28-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your feedback">
    Type your comment and mention a teammate with @ to notify them. To ask Figr directly, pick **Figr · Agent** in the @ list.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Comment box with an @mention list open"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-3.50.25-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply in the thread">
    Open a comment to see the conversation and add your reply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve it when it's done">
    Mark the comment as resolved once the change is made. Resolved comments are hidden from the canvas but stay in the thread history.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Resolving a comment thread"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-3.51.07-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Reviewers outside your workspace?** People who open a shared project link can comment and reply once they sign in. Only workspace members can resolve or delete threads.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Be specific about what should change and why. "Make the CTA more visible, users miss it below the fold" is easier to act on than "fix this".
</Tip>
