*Point at exactly what you mean, right on the canvas.*

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/ZZHGlkyyJgc" title="Figr Canvas: show, point, annotate, then design" frameBorder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowFullScreen />

An annotation marks a region on a screen or image and attaches a note thread to it. Figr reads the marked area together with your note on its next turn, so you can point at the exact spot to change instead of describing where it is.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the annotation tool">
    Pick the annotation tool in the canvas toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the region">
    Drag over the part of the screen or image you want to talk about.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the note">
    Keep it short and specific. For example: "This button should stay disabled until all required fields are filled."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask Figr to act on it">
    Send a message like "Apply the annotated changes." Figr uses the marked area and your note as context.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sticky notes

For quick context that isn't tied to one spot, use the **Sticky Note** tool instead. Drop a note beside the work, like "Need PM input" or "Final direction". Figr reads sticky notes on its next turn too.
