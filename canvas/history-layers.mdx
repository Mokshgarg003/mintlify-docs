Figr keeps two kinds of history. Each item has its own versions, and the whole canvas has a timeline in **Activity**.

## Item version history

Every prototype, doc and flow keeps its own versions. Open the item in **Full screen** and use the version dropdown in the header to look at any earlier state. This changes only that item, not the rest of your canvas.

Use it when:

- A new edit changed too much
- You preferred an earlier direction
- You want to compare two versions

For example, you generate a dashboard, then ask Figr to make it more compact. If the new version feels worse, open the version dropdown and go back to the earlier one.

## Layers

Layers lists everything on the canvas: prototypes, notes, screenshots, flows and sections. Use it to find a specific item, see what belongs inside a section, and move around a crowded canvas faster.

## Activity

Activity is the version history for the whole canvas. Changes are grouped by day and by the prompt that made them, so you can see how the project moved.

To go back, pick a point in the timeline and click **Restore this version**. The canvas returns to how it was at that point.

  <img

<Tip>
  To undo one item, use its version dropdown. To undo a whole round of changes across the canvas, use Activity.
</Tip>
