As a project fills up with screenshots, reviews, flows, PRDs and prototypes, it gets harder to scan. Sticky notes, comments, annotations, sections and connector lines keep it organized.

They also guide Figr. On its next turn, Figr reads the sticky notes, comments, annotations and connector lines on your canvas, so what you mark up becomes context for the next design.

## See what Figr will pick up

A card above the chat box lists the canvas changes you've made since your last message. These are the changes Figr will act on next. Check it before you send.

## Sticky notes

Quick context beside the work: "Need PM input", "Check empty state", "Final direction", "Engineering constraint".

## Comments

Feedback tied to a specific item:

- **@mention** a teammate to pull them in.
- **Resolve** a thread when it's done. Only workspace members can resolve or delete threads.
- **Guests can comment.** People who open a shared project link can comment and reply after signing in, without joining your workspace.
- **Pin comments on prototypes.** On a published prototype, comments stay anchored to a spot on the screen.

## Annotations

Mark a region inside an image and add a note. Figr gets a crop of just that area along with your note, so you can point at exactly what to change.

## Sections

Group related work into named stages, like Research inputs, User flow, Design explorations or Ready for review. Sections behave like Figma frames.

## Connector lines

Show relationships between items, like a PRD connected to its prototype, or a flow connected to the screens it produced.

## Organize canvas automatically

Use **Organize canvas** to group items by creation date or by type: prototypes, documents, flows, images and stickies. Sections you made yourself stay as they are.

## Organize a crowded canvas by hand

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a sticky note">
    Drop a sticky note beside an item to capture quick context or a decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a comment">
    Attach a comment to an item when the feedback belongs to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Group into a section">
    Select related items and group them into a named section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your artifacts">
    Connect related items with connector lines to give Figr the relationship between them.
  </Step>
</Steps>
