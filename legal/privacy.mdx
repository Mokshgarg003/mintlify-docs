Figrfast Systems Private Limited and its affiliate Figr Design Inc. (“Figr”, “Company”, “we” or “us”) respect your privacy, and we are committed to protecting it through this privacy policy (“Privacy Policy”) and our compliance with all applicable laws and related regulations.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, manage and disclose personal information (“Personal Information” or “Personal Data”), and the rights and choices you have in relation to it.

This Privacy Policy applies to the companies and brands that use the Platform to avail the Services (“Customers”), and to anyone else who uses or visits the Platform (collectively, “Users” or “You”).

It should be read together with our Terms of Service and/or the relevant service agreement, order form or Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) signed with the Company.

### **Our Role: Controller and Processor**

**Controller:** We decide how and why we use Personal Data about our own Users and contacts: account details, billing, website visitors, marketing, support, security logs and recruitment. This Privacy Policy covers that data.

**Processor:** When our Customers upload content to Figr (screens, Figma files, documents, design systems, prompts and outputs) (“Service Data”), we process any Personal Data in it only on the Customer’s instructions, under our DPA. If your data is in a Customer’s workspace and you wish to exercise your data protection rights, please contact that Customer first. We will help them respond in accordance with applicable law.

## **Cookies**

Cookies are small files that a site or its service provider transfers to your computer’s hard drive through your web browser, only if you allow, that enable the site’s or service provider’s systems to recognize your browser and capture and remember certain information.

We use cookies to understand and save your preferences for future visits, and to compile aggregate data about site traffic and site interaction so that we can offer better site experiences and tools in the future.

You have the ability to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser setting to decline cookies. If you delete or decline cookies, some features of our website or Platform may not work as designed.

## **User Rights**

Depending on where you live, you have the following rights:

**Right to Access**

You have a right to request a copy of the Personal Information we hold about you, and to be informed of the processing activities we undertake, the identity of the data controller, and the entities or categories of entities to whom your Personal Information may be transferred.

**Right of Rectification**

You have a right to rectify your information in the profile section of the Platform, or request that we rectify inaccurate Personal Information, complete incomplete Personal Information or update it.

**Right to Erasure**

You can request that we erase your Personal Information where: (a) it is no longer needed for the purposes for which it was collected; (b) you have withdrawn your consent; (c) following a successful right to object; (d) it has been processed unlawfully; or (e) to comply with a legal obligation. We are not required to erase Personal Information where processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation or for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

**Right to Restrict Processing**

You have the right to ask us to restrict the processing of your Personal Information.

**Right to Withdraw Consent**

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent shall not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

**Right to Data Portability**

You have the right to receive your Personal Information in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format, and to transmit that data to another controller where technically feasible.

**Right to Object**

You have the right to object to processing of your Personal Information where we rely on a legitimate interest.

**Automated Decision-Making**

You have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect you. We do not make such decisions.

**Right to Unsubscribe**

You can opt out of future e-mails and newsletters at any time by writing to [hi@figr.design](mailto:hi@figr.design) or using the unsubscribe link in the communication. You generally cannot opt out of Service-related announcements.
