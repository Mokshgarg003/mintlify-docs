*Add a public page to your pod so Figr learns your product's content and brand.*

Your marketing site, pricing page or help center already says a lot about your product. Link it to your Context Pod and Figr reads the content, so it knows your features, your language and your audience.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Context Pod">
    Go to **Product intelligence**, open a pod, switch to the **Sources** tab and click **Add sources**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Add sources menu with Website links selected"
        src="/images/docs/web-01-link-website.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the page's URL">
    Choose **Website links** and paste the link. The page must be public, so anyone can open it without logging in.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Website links dialog with a URL pasted and Add link ready"
        src="/images/docs/web-02-url.png"
      />
    </Frame>

    <Info>
      **Want Figr to match how a page looks, not just what it says?** Capture it with the [Figr Capture](/input-research/chrome-extension) Chrome extension instead. A link gives Figr the page's text. A capture gives it the layout and visuals.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to the pod">
    Click **Add link**. Figr reads the page and adds it to your pod's sources.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Sources filtered to Website Links, showing the linked page"
        src="/images/docs/web-03-listed.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  No design system yet? Figr can pick up your brand colours and type from your website automatically.
</Tip>
