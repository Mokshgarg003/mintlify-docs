*Paste a public page into the chat so Figr learns your product's content and brand.*

Your marketing site, pricing page or help center already says a lot about your product. Paste a link into the chat and Figr reads the page, so it knows your features, your language and your audience.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the page's URL">
    Open the page in your browser and copy its link. The page must be public, so anyone can open it without logging in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into the chat box">
    In your project, paste the link into your message.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Public website URL pasted into the Figr chat box with a prompt"
        title="Paste a public page link into the Figr chat"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-22-at-12.48.04-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      No design system yet? Figr can pick up your brand colours and type from your website automatically.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what to do with it">
    Tell Figr how to use the page. For example: "Read our pricing page and design a plan comparison screen that uses the same plan names."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Figr reads the page before it responds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Want Figr to match how a page looks, not just what it says?** Capture it with the [Figr Capture](/input-research/chrome-extension) Chrome extension instead. A link gives Figr the page's text. A capture gives it the layout and visuals.
</Info>
