_Connect your Figma file once, then send any frame to Figr to build on._

When Figr sees your real Figma frames, it builds on your layouts, components and design values instead of starting from scratch. You connect through the Figr plugin once. After that, picking frames takes a few clicks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Figma Sync">
    Click **Figma Sync** in the top-right of the nav bar. This opens the **Figr AI Figma Sync** plugin page.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Figma Sync button in the canvas top-right nav"
        src="/images/docs/fig-01-figma-sync.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the plugin">
    Click **Open in**, run the plugin in your Figma file, then click **Connect to Figr** and approve access in your browser. The plugin shows **Connected** with your email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your frames">
    In Figma, click the frames you want Figr to use. The plugin shows how many are selected.

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 10 21 At 1 18 25 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-1.18.25-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 10 21 At 1 18 25 PM")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send them to Figr">
    Back in Figr, the frames you selected are added to the chat box automatically. Figr brings in each frame's layout, a screenshot and its design values.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Figr chat box with a Figma frame attached after selecting it in Figma"
        src="/images/docs/upd-02-select.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Have a Figma link instead?** Paste it into the chat box as an attachment.
</Info>

<Tip>
  If the plugin shows "0 selected", click a frame directly on the Figma canvas, not in the layers panel.
</Tip>
