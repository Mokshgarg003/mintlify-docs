_Capture any page you can see in Chrome, including your logged-in product, staging or localhost._

Most of your product lives behind a login, where a link can't reach. The Figr Capture extension captures exactly what's on your screen, with its layout and visuals, so Figr can build on the real thing.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/-RgWMfLZIMI" title="Capture a page with Figr Capture" frameBorder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowFullScreen />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the extension">
    In your 

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Figr Capture in the Chrome Web Store"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-1.22.42-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the extension ">
    Add **Figr Capture** from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/figr-capture/bejnddahceggaeogmedenhaccoiikjmj), pin it to your toolbar, and sign in with your Figr account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Log in to your product and go to the page you want Figr to see.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="A logged-in product page with Figr Capture pinned in the toolbar"
        src="/images/image-33.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture it">
    Click the extension, choose **Entire screen** or **Make selection**, then click **Capture**. You can also right-click the page and choose **Capture This Page**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into Figr">
    Click **Copy all captures**, then paste into the chat box in your Figr project.

    <Tip>
      Capture the states that matter, like an empty dashboard or an error screen, not just the happy path.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Capturing several pages?** Captures queue up in the extension. Capture each page in turn, then copy them all at once and paste them into one message.
</Info>
