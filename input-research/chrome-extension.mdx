Most of your product lives behind a login, where a link can't reach. **Figr Capture** captures exactly what's on your screen, with its HTML structure, layout and assets like images, icons and vectors, so Figr builds on the real thing instead of a screenshot.

## Install

1. Add **Figr Capture** from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/figr-capture/bejnddahceggaeogmedenhaccoiikjmj).
2. Pin it to your toolbar.
3. Click the extension and sign in with your Figr account.

## Capture a whole page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Log in to your product and go to the page you want Figr to see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture it">
    Click the extension and choose **Capture This Page**. The capture is copied to your clipboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into Figr">
    Paste into the chat box in your Figr project.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Capture works on production, staging and localhost. Use whichever has the most realistic data.
</Tip>

## Capture part of a page

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/POQmqspcgIs" title="Capture a selection with Figr Capture" frameBorder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowFullScreen />

1. Click the extension and choose **Make selection**.
2. Drag over the area you want.
3. Paste the capture into Figr. Repeat for as many areas as you need.

## Capture several pages or sites

Keep your Figr project open, capture each page in turn, and paste each one into the chat. Use it to gather the states of one flow, or to compare how several products handle the same problem.

Capture the states that matter, like an empty dashboard or an error screen, not just the happy path.

## Using captures

Reference captures in your prompt:

- "Design a settings page that matches the navigation in my dashboard capture."
- "Create a form using the same validation approach as the checkout capture."

To reuse a capture across projects, add it to a [Context Pod](/product-intelligence/context-pods).

## Link or capture?

A [website link in a Context Pod](/product-intelligence/context-pods) gives Figr a public page's text. A capture gives Figr a page's layout and visuals, including pages behind a login.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing pastes into Figr | Capture again, then paste straight away before copying anything else |
| The extension asks you to sign in | Sign in with the same account you use in Figr |
