_Give Figr your PRDs, research and screenshots right in the chat, so it works from your real inputs._

Attach files to a message and Figr reads them before it responds. Use them to ground a design, check a PRD for gaps, or audit a flow from screenshots.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add context in the chat box">
    In your project, click **Add context** in the chat box and choose **Upload from computer**. You can also drag files straight into the chat box.

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 10 22 At 12 33 13 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-22-at-12.33.13-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 10 22 At 12 33 13 PM")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your files">
    Choose PDFs, Markdown or text files, screenshots, CSVs or HTML files. They appear as attachments above your message.

    <Warning>
      Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx) and PowerPoint (.pptx) files aren't supported. Export them to PDF, or paste the text.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what to do with them">
    Tell Figr how to use the files. For example: "Use this PRD and these screenshots to design the new billing settings page."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Figr reads every attachment before it responds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Files you attach in the chat apply to that message. They don't land on the canvas unless you add them there.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Paste text straight into your message for short notes or requirements that don't live in a file.
</Tip>

## Supported files and limits

| Type | Formats |
| --- | --- |
| Images | PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, SVG |
| Video | MP4, MOV, WebM |
| Documents | PDF, Markdown, plain text |
| Data and code | CSV, JSON, CSS, HTML |

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Per file | 100 MB |
| All files in one message | 100 MB |
| PDF pages read per message | 75 |
