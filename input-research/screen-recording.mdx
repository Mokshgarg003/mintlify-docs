A recording shows Figr the whole journey, not just one screen: where people click, what changes and what comes next. Use one when a screenshot can't carry the experience, like a flow, a transition or a state change.

## Record inside Figr

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a recording">
    Choose **Record a walkthrough**, either from the chat box or from **Show us your product** in a Context Pod.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it up">
    Choose the screen or window to share, and turn on your mic if you want to talk through what you're doing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record one flow">
    Walk through the flow from start to finish, the way a user would. Recordings stop automatically after 30 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop and save">
    Stop the recording. Figr adds it to your message or your pod and studies each step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Upload a recording

Already have one? Drag it into the chat box or into **Show us your product** in a Context Pod.

| Formats | Size limit |
| --- | --- |
| .mp4, .mov, .mkv, .webm | 100 MB per file |

<Tip>
  Add a recording to a [Context Pod](/product-intelligence/context-pods) to record a flow once and reuse it in every project.
</Tip>

## What to record

- How a user upgrades their plan
- The current onboarding flow
- Where users get stuck in setup
- How admins manage permissions

Record one flow per video, and say what you're doing and why. Use realistic data, and show the states that matter, like empty, loading or error screens.

## Ask Figr to reason first

Have Figr understand the flow before it designs anything:

```text
Use this recording to understand the current onboarding flow. Identify UX friction, missing states and unclear moments. Then suggest a better flow before designing screens.
```

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Upload fails | Check the format and that the file is under 100 MB |
| Recording stopped early | Recordings stop at 30 minutes. Split long sessions into one flow per video |
