_Show Figr how your product works by recording a flow, so it designs on top of what you already have._

A recording shows Figr the whole journey, not just one screen: where people click, what changes and what comes next. Figr studies each step and keeps it in your Context Pod, so you only record a flow once.

Add it in your respective Context Pod, or can be attached inside the canvas chat agent as an attachment as well.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Context Pod">
    Go to **Product intelligence**, open a pod, switch to the **Sources** tab, click **Add sources** and choose **Capture your screen**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Add sources menu with Capture your screen selected"
        src="/images/docs/rec-01-add-source.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your recording">
    Choose the screen or window to share, and turn on your mic if you want to talk through what you're doing.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Screen recording setup with screen and microphone options"
        src="/images/docs/rec-02-setup.png"
      />
    </Frame>

    <Info>
      **Already have a recording?** Drag it into **Show us your product** instead. Figr accepts .mp4, .mov, .mkv and .webm files up to 100 MB.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record one flow">
    Click **Start recording** and walk through the flow from start to finish, the way a user would. Recordings stop automatically after 30 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop and save">
    Stop the recording. Figr adds it to your pod and studies each screen and step.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Saved recording listed in the pod's sources"
        src="/images/docs/rec-04-saved.png"
      />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      Record one flow per video and say what you're doing and why. Use realistic data, and show the states that matter, like empty, loading or error screens.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>
