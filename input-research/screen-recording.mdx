_Show Figr how your product works by recording a flow, so it designs on top of what you already have._

A recording shows Figr the whole journey, not just one screen: where people click, what changes and what comes next. Record it right from the chat box and Figr uses it for your next message.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a recording from the chat box">
    In your project, click **Add context** in the chat box and choose **Record your screen**. You can also press **⌘⌥S**.

    <Frame>
      ![Image](/images/image-40.png "Image")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your recording">
    Choose the screen or window to share, and turn on your mic if you want to talk through what you're doing.

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 10 22 At 12 30 22 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-22-at-12.30.22-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 10 22 At 12 30 22 PM")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record one flow">
    Walk through the flow from start to finish, the way a user would. Recordings stop automatically after 30 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop and send">
    Stop the recording. It's attached to your message. Add what you want Figr to do with it, like "Audit this onboarding flow", and send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Already have a recording?** Drag it into the chat box instead. Figr accepts .mp4, .mov and .webm files up to 100 MB.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Record one flow per video and say what you're doing and why. Use realistic data, and show the states that matter, like empty, loading or error screens.
</Tip>
