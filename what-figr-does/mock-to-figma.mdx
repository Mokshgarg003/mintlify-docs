With AI, PMs can now put together a first draft of a screen in minutes. But then a designer has to spend one to two days rebuilding it in Figma so the team can actually use it, and that happens on every handoff.

Figr removes the rebuild. The PM's draft goes to Figma as real, editable design.

<Tip>
  **The short version:** A PM brings a rough design, a sketch or a screenshot. Figr turns it into a prototype in your design system, and Export to Figma sends it to Figma as editable layers with auto layout and your variables intact. Designers refine instead of redraw.
</Tip>

**Example on this page:** FreshFold, a laundry pickup and delivery app. Customers book a pickup in the app, a rider collects the bag, and clean clothes come back within 24 hours.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring the first draft">
    Click **Add context** in the chat box and upload a screenshot, a sketch or an AI-generated screen. In the FreshFold example: a PM's rough design of a new pickup confirmation screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Figr rebuilds it in your system">
    Figr turns the draft into a prototype that uses your tokens and components, not the draft's one-off styles.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Rough pickup confirmation draft rebuilt as a prototype in the design system"
        title="Figr rebuilds a draft in your design system"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-2.04.35-AM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export to Figma">
    With the [Figr AI Figma Sync plugin](/export/figma) connected, select the prototype, open **Export**, then **Export to Figma**, and choose **Export this screen** or **Export all screens**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Export to Figma menu on a selected prototype in Figr"
        title="Export a prototype to Figma"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-2.05.19-AM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick up in Figma">
    The design lands in your Figma file as editable layers with auto layout and your variables intact, ready for a designer to refine and hand off.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Exported pickup confirmation screen in Figma with auto layout frames and variables"
        title="An editable Figr export in Figma"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-2.07.58-AM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes for your team

- **PMs** share ideas designers can use, not ideas they have to redraw.
- **Designers** get back the days spent rebuilding.
- **Handoffs** keep your auto layout and variables, so the design stays on system.
