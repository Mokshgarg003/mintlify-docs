A flow can pass every design review and still break once it ships. One team had designs approved, built and released, and only then found that real users couldn't get through the flow. By then, fixing it meant another sprint.

Figr lets you check the flow as people actually use it, then fix it in the same place.

<Note>
  **The short version:** Record a walkthrough of any flow in your product. Figr breaks it into screens, audits each one, pins every issue to the screen it happens on, and redesigns the flow in your design system.
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Record the flow">
    Share your screen and walk through the flow, or upload a recording you already have. In the Ledgerly example: submitting and approving an expense.

    <Info>
      **Asset needed · 2.1**: Screenshot of the screen recording option in the composer, with a Ledgerly recording attached.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Figr breaks it into screens">
    Figr pulls out each screen and step from the recording, so you don't have to screenshot anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Figr audits every screen">
    You get a UX audit with each finding pinned to the exact screen, covering usability, clarity, accessibility and consistency.

    <Info>
      **Asset needed · 2.2**: Screenshot of the audit on the canvas, with findings pinned to three or four Ledgerly screens.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Figr redesigns what's broken">
    Pick the findings that matter, and Figr redesigns those screens in your design system, next to the originals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## See it work

The moment that matters: the same Ledgerly screen before and after, with the audit findings that drove each change.

<Info>

## What changes for your team

- **Designers** catch usability issues before engineering builds them.
- **PMs** get a ranked list of what to fix, tied to real screens.
- **Everyone** reviews the flow people use, not the one in the spec.
