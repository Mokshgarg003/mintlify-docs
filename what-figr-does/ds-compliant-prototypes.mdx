AI-generated designs look good until someone checks them against the design system. Then the questions start: is that our blue, is that our button, why is this card a new component? Teams end up auditing every screen by hand, and soon stop trusting what the AI makes.

Figr builds on your design system from the start, and shows you exactly how closely each prototype follows it.

<Tip>
  **The short version:** Figr builds high-fidelity, clickable prototypes with your tokens and components. It then checks each screen against your system, shows how much of it is compliant, and points to the few elements that aren’t, so you can fix them in one step instead of hunting for them.
</Tip>

**Example on this page:** FreshFold, a laundry pickup and delivery app. Customers book a pickup in the app, a rider collects the bag, and clean clothes come back within 24 hours.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your design system">
    Bring in your tokens and components once. In the FreshFold example: the FreshFold design system with its colors, type, buttons, cards and form fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for the prototype">
    Describe the flow you need. Figr builds high-fidelity, clickable screens using your tokens and components, not lookalikes. In the FreshFold example: the full pickup booking flow, from choosing a slot to payment.

    {/* Asset 8.1: The finished FreshFold booking prototype on the canvas: 5–6 polished screens, clearly on-brand, with the design system chip visible in the composer. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="See how compliant it is">
    Figr checks every screen against your system and shows how much of it follows the design system, with each off-system element marked on the screen.

    {/* Asset 8.2: The compliance view on one FreshFold screen: the compliance percentage, with the 2–3 off-system elements highlighted (for example, a custom grey or a one-off card). */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix it in one step">
    Pick the flagged elements and ask Figr to swap them for the right tokens and components. The screen comes back fully on-system.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes for your team

- **Design system teams** can trust what Figr makes, because they can see the proof on every screen.
- **Designers** stop checking every color and component by hand.
- **Reviews** move from “is this on-system?” to “is this the right design?”
