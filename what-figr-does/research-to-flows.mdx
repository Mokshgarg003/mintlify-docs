Before anyone designs, someone has to pull the context together: interview notes in one doc, a walkthrough on a drive, analytics in another tool. Product teams have to get everything into one place by hand every time.

Figr does that gathering for you, then turns it into the journeys and flows your team designs from.

<Tip>
  **The short version:** Drop in interviews, walkthroughs and notes. Figr reads them together and produces a journey map and the user flows behind it, connected on one canvas, so the team starts design from shared evidence.
</Tip>

**Example on this page:** FreshFold, a laundry pickup and delivery app. Customers book a pickup in the app, a rider collects the bag, and clean clothes come back within 24 hours.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give Figr your research">
    Attach interview notes, a walkthrough recording or research docs with **Add context**, or connect the tools where they live. In the FreshFold example: two interviews with customers who book weekly pickups, and a recording of the booking flow.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Interview notes, a PDF and a walkthrough recording attached in the Figr chat box"
        title="Add research to a Figr chat"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-20-at-11.27.31-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Figr finds the patterns">
    Figr reads every source together and pulls out the goals, steps and pain points that repeat across people.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Figr maps the journey">
    You get a journey map of the experience end to end, with each pain point traced back to where it came from.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Customer journey map for a first laundry pickup with stages and pain points"
        title="A journey map built by Figr from research"
        src="/images/image-11.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Figr turns it into flows">
    Ask for the flows behind any stage, and Figr maps them step by step, ready to design from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Figr writes the PRD">
    Ask for a PRD, and Figr writes the problem, goals, user stories, requirements and edge cases from the same research, with each requirement linked back to the evidence behind it.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="PRD drafted from research next to the journey map and flows on the Figr canvas"
        title="A PRD written by Figr from research"
        src="/images/image-12.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes for your team

- **Researchers** see their findings used, not filed away.
- **PMs** get a journey the whole team agrees on before scoping.
- **Designers** start from real user steps instead of assumptions.
