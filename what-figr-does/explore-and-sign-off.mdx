Most AI design tools give you one answer, or three versions of the same answer in different colors. Good design decisions need real options: directions that take a different approach to the problem, not just a different style.

Figr has the taste to give you those. It’s trained on over 300,000 UX patterns and user psychology principles, and built in partnership with Refero, so every direction is grounded in what works in real products, and comes with the reasoning behind it.

Then Figr helps you land on one. Share a link, collect feedback on the design itself, and keep every decision next to the work.

![Image](/images/image-14.png "Image")

<Tip>
  **The short version:** Ask Figr for several directions at once. You get distinct, well-reasoned approaches, built on 300,000+ UX patterns and user psychology principles, side by side on one canvas. Share one link for review, collect comments on the design, and have Figr apply the final changes, with every decision kept on the canvas.
</Tip>

**Example on this page:** FreshFold, a laundry pickup and delivery app. Customers book a pickup in the app, a rider collects the bag, and clean clothes come back within 24 hours.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for directions">
    Describe the idea and how many directions you want. In the FreshFold example: three takes on the order tracking screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Figr explores with taste">
    Figr draws on proven UX patterns and user psychology principles to pick approaches that are genuinely different, and explains the thinking behind each one.

    {/* Asset 5.1: One FreshFold direction with its reasoning visible: the pattern it uses and the principle behind it (for example, a progress timeline to reduce "where's my order?" anxiety). */}

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 21 At 12 01 52 AM](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-12.01.52-AM.png "Screenshot 2026 10 21 At 12 01 52 AM")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare them side by side">
    Each direction sits on the canvas next to the others. Take any one further in its own thread without losing the rest.

    ![Image](/images/image-16.png "Image")

    {/* Asset 5.2: Three FreshFold order tracking directions side by side on the canvas, labelled: map-first, timeline-first and minimal. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share one link for review">
    Send stakeholders a link. They comment on the design itself, not in a separate thread.

    ![Image](/images/image-17.png "Image")

    {/* Asset 5.3: The shared review link with 2–3 comments pinned on the chosen FreshFold direction. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Figr acts on the feedback">
    Ask Figr to apply the final changes, and it updates the design. The comment, the change and the reason stay together.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes for your team

- **Designers** get directions with real range, in the time one used to take.
- **Stakeholders** review the design itself, from one link.
- **Everyone** can see why a decision was made, months later.
