Generating a screen was never the hard part. The hard part is fitting new work into a product that already exists: finding the right screens, using your design system, covering every state, and handing off something engineering can actually build.

That's the work Figr does. It learns your product once, then designs inside it.

<Info>
  **Asset needed · 0.1**: Hero image or 20–30s loop. One Ledgerly project on the Figr canvas showing the full chain: a walkthrough recording and a Context Pod on the left, a user flow and prototype in the middle, a Figma export and an MCP link on the right.
</Info>

<Note>
  **Share this with your team:** Figr is an AI product design agent that works inside our existing product. It reads our screens, flows and design system, then designs new features, states and flows that fit, and hands them off to Figma or straight into engineering's tools.
</Note>

## Why teams pick Figr

- **It starts from your product.** Figr works from your screens, recordings, docs and design system, not a blank prompt.
- **It covers the whole job.** Research, flows, states, prototypes and handoff live on one canvas, connected to each other.
- **It fits how your team already works.** Designs go to Figma, code goes to your engineers' agents, and reviews happen on a shared link.
