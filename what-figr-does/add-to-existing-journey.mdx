Most AI design tools start from a blank page. Your work doesn't. A new feature has to land in a journey that already ships, on screens your users already know, in a design system you already maintain.

That's where generic tools stall. Teams tell us their AI agent doesn't know which screen to edit, and that the states each screen needs only get defined when engineering asks for them.

<Note>
  **The short version:** Tell Figr the feature. It finds the screens in your journey that the feature touches, designs the change with your components, then adds the empty, loading and error states around it. You review one connected flow instead of stitching screens together.
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the feature">
    One or two lines about what you're adding and who it's for. In the Ledgerly example: bulk receipt upload when submitting expenses.

    <Info>
      **Asset needed · 4.1**: Screenshot of the composer with the bulk-upload prompt typed in and the Ledgerly Context Pod and design system attached.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Figr finds where it belongs">
    Figr uses what it knows about your product to find the screens in the journey the feature touches. You don't point it at files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Figr designs it in your system">
    The new screens use your tokens, components and spacing, so they look like part of the product from the first pass.

    <Info>
      **Asset needed · 4.2**: Screenshot of the canvas showing the existing Ledgerly submit-expense screens next to the new bulk-upload screens, visibly using the same components.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Figr fills in every state">
    A follow-up pass adds the empty, loading, error and permission states for each screen it changed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## See it work

The moment that matters: Figr places the feature in the right screens on its own, then a second pass adds the states most tools leave out.

<Info>
  **Asset needed · 4.3 (proof moment)**: 20–30s clip. The prompt is sent, Figr highlights the Ledgerly screens it will change, the new screens appear, then the follow-up adds empty, uploading, partial-failure and success states beside them.
</Info>

## What changes for your team

- **Designers** start from your product, not a blank frame.
- **PMs** see the full journey, states included, before scoping.
- **Engineers** get the states they'd otherwise ask about mid-sprint.
