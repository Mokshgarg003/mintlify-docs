Many teams don't have a design system yet. They have products. One team had four apps, each built independently, with four versions of every button. Another had about 70 patterns in use that nobody had ever written down.

Starting a design system from scratch takes months. Figr starts from what you already ship.

<Note>
  **The short version:** Upload screens from the products you already have. Figr finds the colors, type, spacing, components and patterns they share, and turns them into one design system your team can review, edit and use in every design Figr makes.
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your screens">
    Add screenshots from every product, plus any CSS or style files you have. In the Ledgerly example: screens from the web app, mobile app, admin console and card app.

    <Info>
      **Asset needed · 9.1**: Screenshot of the Design Language setup screen with 15–20 Ledgerly screenshots from the four apps uploaded.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Figr finds what's shared">
    Figr compares every screen and pulls out the colors, type scale, spacing, components and patterns they have in common.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the system">
    You get one structured design system, with each token labelled by where it's used. Edit anything before you save it.

    <Info>
      **Asset needed · 9.2**: Screenshot of the generated Ledgerly design language: token categories (typography, brand colors, neutrals) with usage comments, plus a few extracted components.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design with it">
    From then on, every screen Figr makes uses the new system, across all your products.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes for your team

- **Design leads** get a working system in days, not a quarter.
- **Undocumented patterns** finally get names and owners.
- **Every product** starts to look like one product.
