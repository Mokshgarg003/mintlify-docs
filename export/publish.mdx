Publish and share is how people see your work outside your canvas. Publish one prototype as a live link, or share the whole project with your team.

## Publish a prototype

<Frame caption="The publish panel">
  <img
    alt="Publish panel showing the public link, the published version and its status"
    src="/images/Publish-1.avif"
  />
</Frame>

Publishing turns one prototype into a link people can open without seeing the rest of your canvas. Use it to send a direction to a stakeholder, or to put a prototype in front of users.

- **The link is pinned to a version.** It shows the exact version you published. Later edits don't change it.
- **Update when you're ready.** The publish panel shows which version is live and flags when a newer one exists. Click **Update to latest** to move the link forward.
- **The live link stays up while you update.** The new build finishes in the background, and the current link keeps working until it's done.
- **Failed builds are visible.** If a build fails, the panel says so and lets you retry.
- **Unpublish takes the link down.** The page stops loading for anyone who has it.
- **Reviewers can comment on it.** Comments on a published prototype are pinned to a spot on the screen and stay there as reviewers pan and zoom.

<Note>
  Password protection for shared links is available on the Max plan. Members with view-only access can't publish.
</Note>

## Share a project

Share the whole project when someone needs the canvas, not just one prototype: a designer refining outputs, a PM reviewing flows and PRDs, or an engineer checking implementation context.

<Frame caption="Project share settings">
  <img
    alt="Project share modal showing who has access and their access level"
    src="/images/Share-1.avif"
  />
</Frame>

Choose who can open the project:

| Access | Who it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Only invited people | People you add by email |
| Everyone in your workspace | All members of your workspace |
| Anyone with the link | Anyone who has the URL |

Each option can be set to **Can view** or **Can edit**.

People who open a shared project without joining your workspace can still comment and reply once they sign in. Only workspace members can resolve or delete comment threads.
