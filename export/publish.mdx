_Let anyone click through your prototype, no Figr account needed._

Publish a prototype to get a link you can send to stakeholders, testers or customers. They open it in a browser and use it like a real product, without seeing your canvas or chats.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the prototype and click Publish">
    Double-click the prototype to open it in full screen, then choose **Publish**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Publish option on a prototype in full screen"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-4.03.08-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    Figr creates a link to the live prototype. Copy it and send it to anyone.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Published prototype link with a copy button"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-4.03.51-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update it when you make changes">
    The link stays on the version you published, so later edits don't show until you choose. When you're ready, click **Publish latest version** so the link shows the newest version. The current link keeps working while the update builds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a published link for user testing. Testers see only the prototype, so nothing on your canvas distracts them.
</Tip>

## Good to know

- **Unpublish anytime.** Unpublishing takes the link down for everyone who has it.
- **Let viewers comment.** Turn on **Viewers can leave comments on this link** in the publish panel. It's off by default. Comments are pinned to a spot on the screen.
- **Password protection** is available on the Max plan.
- **View-only members can't publish.** Ask someone with edit access to the project.
