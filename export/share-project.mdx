*Bring your team into the work.*

Share a project to let teammates see the canvas, follow the chats, leave comments and keep building with Figr. Everyone works on the same canvas, so there's one place for the latest version.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the project and click Share">
    Go to the project you want to share and click **Share** in the top-right corner.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Share button in the top-right of a project"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-4.04.58-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite people">
    Enter your teammates' emails and choose what they can do: **Can view** or **Can edit**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Share window with an email entered and the access level picker"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-4.05.36-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>

    <Note>
      **Can edit** is available on the Max plan and above. Inviting people isn't available on older Free and Starter plans.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Teammates get an email with a link to the project. Anyone already in your workspace can also find it in their project list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose who can open it

| Access | Who it covers | Access level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Only invited people | People you add by email | Set per person when you invite them |
| Everyone in your workspace | All members of your workspace | Can view or Can edit |
| Anyone with the link | Anyone who has the URL | Can view or Can edit |

<Frame>
  <img
    alt="General access options in the share window"
    src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-4.05.59-PM.png"

</Frame>

<Info>
  **Reviewers outside your workspace?** People who open a shared project link can comment and reply once they sign in, without joining your workspace. Only workspace members can resolve or delete comment threads.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Share the Context Pod and design system along with the project, so teammates get the same on-brand results from Figr that you do.
</Tip>
