*Give QA a ready list of what to test, straight from your designs.*

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/TeR251naXr4" title="Generate PRDs, test cases and product documentation with Figr AI" frameBorder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowFullScreen />

Figr turns a flow, prototype or PRD into test cases your QA team can use. Each case covers what to do, what should happen, and which edge cases to check, so testing starts from the design instead of guesswork.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick what to test">
    Select a flow, prototype or PRD on your canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add any rules Figr should know">
    If your product has rules or roles that aren't in the design, like who can approve what, add them with **Add context** in the chat box, as a PRD or a short note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask Figr for test cases">
    For example: "Write test cases for this sign-up flow, including edge cases."

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Test case prompt in the Figr chat box with a flow attached"
        title="Ask Figr to write test cases"
        src="/images/docs/test-03-prompt-1.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the test cases">
    Figr lists each case with steps and the expected result. Remove what's out of scope and add anything specific to your setup.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Generated test cases with steps and expected results on the Figr canvas"
        title="Test cases generated by Figr"
        src="/images/docs/test-04-list-1.png"
      />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      Map edge cases first, then generate test cases. Figr turns each edge case into a test, so nothing you designed for goes untested.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand them to QA">
    Share the test cases with your team, or copy them into your testing tool.
  </Step>
</Steps>
