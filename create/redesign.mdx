*Improve a screen you already have, without losing what works.*

Redesign is one of the most common things teams use Figr for. Start from your current screen, tell Figr what to change and what to keep, and compare the result side by side with the original.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring in the current screen">
    Click **Add context** in the chat box and add a screenshot, capture the page with [Figr Capture](/input-research/chrome-extension), bring in [frames from Figma](/input-research/figma-input), or attach a [product walkthrough](/input-research/screen-recording).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your design system">
    Choose your design system in the chat box so the redesign stays on-brand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what to improve and what to keep">
    Be specific about the problem and the guardrails. For example: "Make this settings page easier to scan. Keep our navigation and existing components."

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Redesign prompt typed in the Figr chat box with the current screen attached"
        title="Ask Figr to redesign a screen"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-3.14.52-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      Name one goal per prompt. "Reduce clutter" and "add a new filter" work better as two steps than one.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare before and after">
    Figr places the redesign next to your original on the canvas so you can review the changes side by side.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Original screen and Figr's redesign side by side on the canvas"
        title="Compare a redesign with the original"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-3.15.20-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine the details">
    Use [Manual edit](/getting-started/tutorial/untitled-page-5) to change a specific section, or ask for another variation in chat.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Editing one section of the redesigned screen in Edit mode"
        title="Refine a redesign with manual edit"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-3.16.01-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Not sure what to fix?** Ask Figr for a [UX review](/getting-started/tutorial/review) of the current screen first. It pins issues to the screen and ranks them, so you know where to start.
</Info>
