*Improve a screen you already have, without losing what works.*

Redesign is one of the most common things teams use Figr for. Start from your current screen, tell Figr what to change and what to keep, and compare the result side by side with the original.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring in the current screen">
    Upload a screenshot, capture the page with [Figr Capture](/input-research/chrome-extension), add [frames from Figma](/input-research/figma-input), or use a [product walkthrough](/input-research/screen-recording).

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="A screenshot of the current screen attached in the chat box"
        src="/images/docs/re-01-current.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach your design system and pod">
    Select your design system and Context Pod in the chat so the redesign stays on-brand and fits your product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what to improve and what to keep">
    Be specific about the problem and the guardrails. For example: "Make this settings page easier to scan. Keep our navigation and existing components."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare before and after">
    Figr places the redesign next to your original on the canvas so you can review the changes side by side.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="The original screen and the redesign side by side on the canvas"
        src="/images/docs/re-04-compare.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine the details">
    Use the [manual edit](/getting-started/tutorial/untitled-page-5) panel to change a specific section, or ask for another variation in chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Not sure what to fix?** Ask Figr for a [UX review](/getting-started/tutorial/review) of the current screen first. It pins issues to the screen and ranks them, so you know where to start.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Name one goal per prompt. "Reduce clutter" and "add a new filter" work better as two steps than one.
</Tip>
