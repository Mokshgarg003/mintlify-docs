## What is Figr AI?

Figr AI is an AI product designer that learns your product before it designs. Give it your screens, recordings, docs and design system, and it creates flows, prototypes, PRDs and design reviews that fit what you already ship.

## How to use this help center

- **Search:** Know what you’re looking for? Use the search bar at the top to quickly find answers. Try keywords like Context Pod, design system, screen recording, Figma export or share.
- **Browse:** Prefer to explore? Navigate through the left sidebar to read topics step by step and discover everything Figr can do.
