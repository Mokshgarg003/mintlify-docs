Product-aware means Figr designs around your product, not a generic idea of what an app should look like. Three parts make that possible, and they work together:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Context Pod" href="/product-intelligence/context-pods">

  <Card title="Design System" href="/product-intelligence/design-system">

  </Card>

  </Card>

## Why the combination matters

A Context Pod tells Figr what your product is and what you're building. Your Design System tells it how the product looks. The canvas is where you brainstorm, review and refine. The result is design grounded in your product, not a generic guess.
