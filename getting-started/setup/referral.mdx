Credits power every generation in Figr. On self-serve plans, open **Earn more credits** at the bottom of the left sidebar to see your tasks and claim rewards.

<Note>
  Earning credits isn't available on Enterprise plans. Ask your workspace admin for more credits instead.
</Note>

<Frame caption="The Earn more credits panel, opened from the sidebar.">
  ![The Earn more credits panel](/images/earn-credits.png)
</Frame>

## Ways to earn

| Task | What you do | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set up your design system | Connect Figma or add another source so Figr learns your tokens and components | 5 credits |
| Input live product with Chrome extension | Capture a page of your product with [Figr Capture](/input-research/chrome-extension) and add it to Figr. Installing alone doesn't count | 5 credits |
| Review on G2 or Product Hunt | Share honest feedback about Figr | 5 credits |
| Share on X or LinkedIn | Post about Figr and claim your credits | 10 credits |

Each task credits your balance once. Set up your design system from the [Design System](/product-intelligence/design-system) page. The rest are one click from the Earn more credits panel.
