The quick path from signup to a generated prototype. For the full setup, see [Add product context](/getting-started/setup/add-product-context).

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/q3boC0Rxyx4" title="Build your first prototype with Figr" frameBorder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowFullScreen />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up and create a canvas">
    Sign up at [figr.design](https://figr.design), finish onboarding, then click **New canvas**. You can pick a Context Pod and a design system for the canvas as you create it, so Figr starts with your product's context and components.

    <Frame caption="Start a new canvas">
      ![The new canvas screen](/images/02-01-start-new-canvas-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a little product context">
    Bring in one screen, recording, doc or link so Figr understands your product. A single source already makes the output feel like your product instead of a blank-slate prompt.

    <Frame caption="Add one source to make Figr product-aware">
      ![Add your product details screen with the five source options](/images/docs/cp-03-sources.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what you want">
    Write a short prompt for the screen or flow you need, and attach any context. Say who it's for, what they need to do, and which states matter.

    <Frame caption="Chat box with attachments">
      ![Chat box with attachment options](/images/canvas-chat-reference.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Figr design">
    Figr reasons through the flow and builds a high-fidelity prototype in your design system.

    <Frame caption="Generated prototype">
      ![A generated high-fidelity prototype](/images/High-Fidelity-Prototype.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and refine">
    Ask for changes in chat, or click **Edit** on the prototype to change one part without regenerating the rest. See [Edit without regenerating](/getting-started/tutorial/untitled-page-5).

    <Frame caption="Refine in chat or edit in place">
      ![Edit panel showing the properties of a selected element](/direct-edit.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export or share">
    Send screens to Figma, publish a link, download images or code, or open the design in a coding agent. See [Export options](/export/options).

    <Frame caption="Export and share options">
      <img
        alt="Export menu with Figma, link, image and code options"
        src="/images/ChatGPT-Image-Jun-25,-2026,-04_31_09-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The more product context you add, the closer the output matches your product. Once you've run through this, set up your full [product context](/product-intelligence/context-pods) and [design system](/product-intelligence/design-system).
</Tip>
