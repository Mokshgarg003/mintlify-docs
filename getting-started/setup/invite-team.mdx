Bring your team into your Figr workspace so everyone works from the same Context Pods, design systems and projects.

## Invite a teammate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your profile icon at the bottom left, then open **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Members & Roles">
    Open the **Members & Roles** tab and click **Invite members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    In the **Invite Member** dialog, enter one email address and click **Send Invitation**. Repeat for each person.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your teammate gets an email with a link to join your workspace. Once they join, they can open anything shared with the workspace and start their own projects.

## Let people join with your work domain

You don't have to invite everyone by hand. People who sign up with your company's email domain can find your workspace and ask to join.

- **Join requests.** Someone with a matching work email requests access. Admins approve it from the email Figr sends or from **Settings → Members & Roles**.
- **Auto-join.** Turn on auto-join to let anyone with your domain join instantly, without approval.

Only admins can change these settings.
