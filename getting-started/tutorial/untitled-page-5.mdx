_Change your prototype yourself, or describe the change and let Figr make it._

There are two ways to edit a prototype in Figr. Edit by hand for quick fixes you can see, like copy, colour and spacing. Edit with a prompt for changes to how the prototype works or how it's laid out, especially when they affect more than one screen.

## Edit by Hand:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a prototype in full screen by double clicking on it.">
    Find the screen on your canvas and double-click it to open full screen. Switch to the **Edit** tab.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Interact, Edit and Comment tabs with Edit selected"
        src="/images/docs/edit-01-tab.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the element you want to change">
    Select any text, button, image or container on the screen. Its properties appear in the panel.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Edit panel showing the properties of a selected element"
        src="/direct-edit.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your change">
    Type to update text, or change colour, size, spacing and alignment in the properties panel.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Properties panel with the fill colour picker open"
        src="/images/docs/edit-03-colour.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prompt the changes">
    Select a component, type the edit you want in the prompt box, and send it with your other queued changes.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Element prompt box with queued changes and Apply changes"
        src="/images/docs/edit-04-prompt.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Your change shows on the screen right away. Click another element to keep editing, or close the editor when you're done. If you try to leave with changes you haven't applied, Figr warns you first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit with Prompt:

Describe the change in chat and Figr makes it for you. Use prompts for:

- **New behaviour:** "When I click Delete, show a confirmation popup."
- **New screens or states:** "Add an empty state for when there are no projects yet."
- **Layout changes:** "Move the filters from the top bar into a left sidebar."
- **Changes across screens:** "Rename 'Workspace' to 'Team' everywhere."

![Screenshot 2026 10 21 At 3 06 33 AM](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-3.06.33-AM.png "Screenshot 2026 10 21 At 3 06 33 AM")

When a change affects structure, like navigation or a layout pattern, Figr applies it everywhere it's used in the prototype, so every screen stays consistent.
