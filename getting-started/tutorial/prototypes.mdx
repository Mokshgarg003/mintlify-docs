_Describe what you want to build and get a clickable, high-fidelity prototype you can test and share._

Figr turns a short description into a working prototype on your canvas: real screens, real interactions and your design system, not a static mockup. You can click through it, show it to stakeholders, and keep refining it in chat.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your context">
    In the chat box, attach your Context Pod and design system, plus any screenshots, recordings or Figma frames that show where this fits in your product.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Add context menu in the chat box with a design system attached"
        src="/images/docs/proto-01-context.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what to build">
    Say who it's for, what they need to do, and where it fits. For example: "Build a mobile checkout for returning customers. They should be able to pay with a saved card in two taps."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer any questions">
    Figr may ask a few questions to fill gaps before it starts. Answer them, or tell it to go ahead with its best guess.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Figr asking clarifying questions before it starts building"
        src="/images/docs/proto-03-questions.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click through your prototype">
    The prototype appears on your canvas. Click **Interact** to try it, or **Full screen** to see it at device size.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="A generated prototype on the canvas"
        src="/images/prototypes.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for the states">
    Ask Figr to design the states a screen needs. For example: "Add empty, loading, error and success states for the order history screen."

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="One screen designed in loading, success, empty and error states"
        src="/images/docs/proto-05-states.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine it">
    Ask for changes in chat, or [edit manually](/getting-started/tutorial/untitled-page-5) to change just one part without regenerating the rest.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="A follow-up refinement prompt in the chat box"
        src="/images/docs/proto-06-refine.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/q3boC0Rxyx4" title="Your first prototype: from product problem to Figma-ready prototype" frameBorder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowFullScreen />

<Tip>
  The more specific your first message, the better the first version. Mention the user, the goal and the states that matter, like empty, loading or error.
</Tip>
