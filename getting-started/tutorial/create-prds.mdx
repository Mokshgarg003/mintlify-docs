_Write product docs that already know your product._

Figr can draft PRDs, feature specs and other product docs using what's in your Context Pod. Because it knows your product, the first draft starts closer to what you'd write yourself, and you spend your time sharpening it instead of filling a blank page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Attach your Context Pod">
    Select your pod in the chat so the doc reflects how your product works today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what you need">
    Say what the doc is for, who it's for, and what you already know. For example: "Write a PRD for bulk-inviting teammates. The goal is to cut onboarding time for teams of 10 or more."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add supporting material">
    Attach research notes, customer feedback, or a link from a connected tool like Notion or Linear so the doc builds on real inputs.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Research notes attached to the chat box as supporting material"
        src="/images/docs/prd-03-material.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    Figr creates the doc on the canvas with sections like problem, goals, user stories and requirements.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="A PRD connected to its flow and prototype on the canvas"
        src="/images/PRD.avif"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine it">
    Edit the doc directly, or ask Figr to expand a section, tighten the scope, or add open questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask Figr to map the flow">
    For example: "Map the user flow for this PRD, including what happens when something goes wrong."
  </Step>
</Steps>

  Tell Figr what's out of scope. A clear "not doing" list keeps the draft focused.
</Tip>
