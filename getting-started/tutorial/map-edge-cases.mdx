_Find what can go wrong before your users do._

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/1hAOPDLPCuU" title="Map UX edge cases and user flows with Figr AI" frameBorder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowFullScreen />

Edge cases are the situations a happy-path design misses: empty states, errors, slow connections, permissions, long names, and users who do things in an unexpected order. Figr looks at your screen or flow and lists the cases worth designing for.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring in what you want to check">
    Select a screen, flow or prototype on your canvas, or upload a screenshot of a live screen.

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 10 21 At 3 30 50 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-3.30.50-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 10 21 At 3 30 50 PM")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach your Context Pod">
    Select your pod in the chat so Figr knows your users, roles and product rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask Figr to map the edge cases">
    For example: "What edge cases should we design for on this checkout flow?"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the list">
    Figr groups the cases by type, such as empty states, errors, permissions and data limits. Decide which ones need a design.

    <Frame caption="Figr maps the happy path and every state around it.">
      <img
        alt="Happy path versus a full edge-case map"
        src="/images/Map-Edge-Cases.avif"
      />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      Map edge cases before handoff, not after. It's cheaper to design an error state now than to fix it once it ships.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design the ones that matter">
    Ask Figr to design the screens or states for the cases you picked. For example: "Design the payment failed and expired card states."
  </Step>
</Steps>
