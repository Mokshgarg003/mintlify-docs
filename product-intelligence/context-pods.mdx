A Context Pod holds everything Figr knows about your product: how it works, how it looks in use, and the words your team uses. Where your [Design System](/product-intelligence/design-system) defines how things look, a Context Pod captures what your product is and how it behaves: flows, terminology, user needs and constraints.

Build it once, share it with your team, and attach it to any canvas.

## Create a Context Pod

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Product intelligence">
    In the sidebar, open **Product intelligence**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new pod">
    Click **Create new Context Pod** in the top right. Name it after one product area, like _Checkout_ or _Admin dashboard_, and add a one-line description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add at least one source">
    On **Add your product details**, choose how to show Figr your product. See the source types below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the pod">
    Click **Start Building**. Figr reads your sources and shows a summary of what it learned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add your sources

Use as many source types as you like. The more you add, the deeper Figr's understanding.

### Show us your product (recommended)

Record a walkthrough of a flow, with optional mic narration, or drop in an existing recording (.mp4, .mov, .mkv or .webm, up to 100 MB). A recording tells Figr how screens connect and how people move through them, which a screenshot can't. See [Screen recordings](/input-research/screen-recording).

### Upload files

Add PRDs, specs, research and screenshots. See [Files and research](/input-research/files-research) for supported types.

### Paste text

Open the note editor, give it a title and write. Useful for product overviews, terminology or notes that don't live in a file.

### Link website

Paste the URL of a public page, like your marketing site or help center. Figr reads the page's content. To capture a page behind a login, use [Figr Capture](/input-research/chrome-extension) instead.

### Add instructions

Rules that shape how Figr responds:

```text
We call customers "members", never "users".
```

```text
Prioritize mobile patterns.
```

## The pod summary

After building, you land on the pod's summary. A drawer on the right lists every source linked to the pod, with its status. Come back anytime to review what the pod knows, or to add and remove sources from the **Sources** tab.

## Use a Context Pod

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Start a canvas from a pod">
    Open a pod and start a new canvas from it. The pod is already attached, so your first message has the product context.

    You can also pick a pod when you create a canvas with **New canvas**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Attach it in a chat">
    1. In the chat box, open the attachment menu and choose **Context Pod**.
    2. Pick your pod. It appears as a chip above your message.

## What a pod remembers

A pod gets better as you work with it.

- **Past chats.** New chats can pull relevant decisions from earlier chats that used the same pod, and show where they came from. If nothing relevant exists, Figr doesn't add anything.
- **Memory.** Figr remembers product decisions, constraints and preferences across your work in a pod, and shows which memories or sources it used for a message.

## Share a pod with your team

When your team shares one pod, everyone starts from the same understanding of your product. Nobody records the same flow or uploads the same PRD again.

1. Open the pod in **Product intelligence** and click **Share** in the top right.
2. Teammates attach it from the **Context Pod** picker in any project.
3. When your product changes, replace old recordings and links in the **Sources** tab so everyone works from what's real.

## Context Pod vs Design System

Most teams use both.

|  | Context Pod | Design System |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Captures | What your product is and how it works | How your product looks |
| Sources | Recordings, files, text, links, instructions | Figma, Storybook, npm, code, brand assets |
| Shapes | Flows, terminology, behavior, content | Colours, typography, spacing, components |

## Best practices

<Tip>
  - Make one pod per product area. A focused pod gives sharper results than one pod for everything.
  - Lead with a recording. A walkthrough shows flow and behavior that files alone can't.
  - Keep instructions specific. "Use our terms" or "mobile-first" steers output more than vague guidance.
  - Name pods so teammates can tell them apart, like "Web app – Checkout" and "Mobile app – Onboarding".
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Website link won't attach | Check the URL is valid and public |
| Pod doesn't influence a response | Attach it as a chip before you send the message |
| Recording upload fails | Check it's .mp4, .mov, .mkv or .webm and under 100 MB |
| Output ignores your terminology | Add an instruction spelling out the terms to use |
