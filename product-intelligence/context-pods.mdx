_Give Figr one place to learn your product, then reuse it in every project._

A Context Pod holds everything Figr knows about your product: how it works, how it looks, and the words your team uses. Attach it to a project and Figr designs from your real product instead of guessing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a pod">
    In the sidebar, open **Product intelligence** and click **Create new** in the top right.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Create new button on the Context Pod page"
        src="/images/docs/cp-01-create.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it after one product area">
    Give the pod a clear name, like _Checkout_ or _Admin dashboard_, and add a one-line description so teammates know what's inside.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add at least one source">
    On **Add your product details**, choose how to show Figr your product:

    - **Show us your product:** record a walkthrough of a flow, or drop in an existing recording
    - **Upload files:** PRDs, specs, research or screenshots
    - **Paste text:** notes or requirements
    - **Link website:** a public page for Figr to read
    - **Add instructions:** rules Figr should always follow

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Add your product details screen with the five source options"
        src="/images/docs/cp-03-sources.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the pod">
    Click **Start Building**. Figr reads your sources and shows a summary of what it learned. You can add or remove sources anytime from the drawer on the right.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Context Pod summary with linked sources in the right drawer"
        src="/images/context-pod-summary.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach it to your chat">
    In a project, click **Add context** in the chat box, choose **Context Pod**, and pick your pod. It appears as a chip above your message.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Context Pod picker in the chat box"
        src="/images/context-pod-menu-(3).png"
        className="dark:hidden"
      />
      <img
        alt="Context Pod picker in the chat box"
        src="/images/context-pod-menu-1.png"
        className="hidden dark:block"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/ABHB0Jp_k9k" title="Context Pod: teach Figr AI everything about your product" frameBorder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowFullScreen />

<Note>
  Attach the pod before you send your message. Figr only uses it for messages sent after it's attached.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Make one pod per product area. A focused pod gives sharper results than one pod for everything.
</Tip>
