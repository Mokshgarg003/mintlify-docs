_Bring live context from the tools your team already uses._

Your specs live in Notion, your tickets in Linear, your numbers in Mixpanel. Connect them and Figr can pull what it needs while it works, instead of you copying and pasting it in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your connectors">
    In a project, click **Add context** in the chat box, then **Connectors → Manage connectors**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Manage connectors in the chat box"
        src="/images/connectors-console.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tool">
    Choose the tool you want to connect, such as Notion, Confluence, Google Workspace, Linear, Asana, GitHub, Slack, Miro, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Granola or Mobbin.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="List of available connectors"
        src="/images/connectors-list.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and allow access">
    Click **Connect**, sign in to the tool, review the permissions it asks for, and click **Authorize**. A green **Connected** badge appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on or off">
    Use the toggle next to each connector to choose which ones Figr can use in your chats.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Connector toggles in the chat box"
        src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-21-at-1.35.53-PM.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Each member connects their own accounts.** Connecting a tool gives Figr access with your account only, not your teammates'.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Check the permissions.** Some connectors ask for write access as well as read, for example to Notion pages, Slack messages and canvases, or Miro boards. Review what each tool asks for before you authorize it.
</Warning>

<Info>
  **Using a tool that isn't listed?** Click **Add custom**, enter its name, MCP server URL and transport type, then click **Add connector** and **Connect**.
</Info>

<Tip>
  For GitHub, choose only the repos Figr should read. Connect only the tools you'll actually use.
</Tip>

**Stuck?** If the sign-in page doesn't come back to Figr, log in to the tool in the same browser first, then try again.
