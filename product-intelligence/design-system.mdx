_Bring your Figma variables, styles and components into Figr, so every screen it makes looks like your product._

With your design system in Figr, every screen uses your real colours, type and components from the first message. You spend less time fixing off-brand drafts and more time on the design itself.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/oGHOvoOuvqE" title="Introducing Figr's Design System Intelligence" frameBorder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowFullScreen />

<Warning>
  **Before you start:** your colours, type and spacing need to be set up as variables or styles in Figma. Figr can't pick up values that only live on individual layers.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new design system">
    In the sidebar, open **Design System** and create a new one. Give it a name and choose whether it's just for you or shared with your workspace.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Create Design System form with name and sharing options"
        src="/images/docs/ds-01-new.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Figma plugin">
    Under **Sources**, choose **Figma**, then click **Open plugin**. Run **Figr AI Figma Sync** in your design system file and check that it shows **Connected**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Continue in the Figma plugin dialog naming the design system file"
        src="/images/docs/ds-02-plugin.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to bring in">
    Select what Figr should learn from: variables, styles, fonts, components, reference screens, icons, logos and images.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Plugin step for choosing reference screens to import"
        src="/images/docs/ds-03-choose.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it up with Figr">
    Click **Setup design system**. Setup runs as a chat with Figr: it reads your selection, asks you a few questions about how your system is used, then builds your design system. Answer them, or tell it to go ahead with its best guess.

    <Frame>
      <img
        alt="Design system tokens after setup"
        src="/images/docs/ds-04-result.png"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Include a few reference screens along with your components. They show Figr how your components are used together, not just what they look like.
</Tip>

## Other sources

You can also build your design system from [Storybook](/design-system/storybook), an [npm package](/design-system/npm-package), your [code repo](/design-system/code-repo) or [brand assets](/design-system/brand-assets). Combine Figma with a code source and Figr matches your Figma components with their code in one design system.
